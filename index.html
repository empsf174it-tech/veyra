Aftercare

Protect your results

Essential steps to care for your skin after treatment.

General post-treatment timeline

Day 0 · treatment day

Avoid touching face, sweating, or applying makeup.

Day 1–3

Use gentle cleanser and provided barrier cream. Strict SPF.

Week 1+

Gradually reintroduce active ingredients as advised.

Warning signs

Contact the clinic if you experience severe pain, spreading redness, blistering, or signs of infection.

Your daily checklist

Chemical peel aftercare

Crucial: do not pick, peel, or scrub shedding skin. Allow it to flake naturally to prevent scarring.

  • Use only cool water to wash.
  • Avoid excessive heat (saunas, hot yoga) for 7 days.

Laser treatment aftercare

Skin may feel like a mild sunburn. Cool compresses are acceptable.

  • Strict sun avoidance for 2–4 weeks post-treatment.
  • Avoid active skincare (retinols, AHAs) until cleared by your expert.
